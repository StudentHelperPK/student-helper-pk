GPA & CGPA

Understanding GPA

1️⃣

GPA

GPA ka matlab Grade Point Average hai. Ye normally ek semester ke academic performance ko represent karta hai.

2️⃣

CGPA

CGPA multiple semesters ke academic performance ka cumulative average hota hai.

3️⃣

Credit Hours

Credit hours courses ke academic workload ko represent karte hain aur GPA calculation mein important role play karte hain.
